## About Tidewell

We started in 2012 in a garage with one question: why do household basics break so quickly?

Today we're a team of 40 designers, buyers and support staff. We test every product ourselves, work directly with small factories, and answer every customer by name. If something isn't right, we fix it.

[Talk to our team](#contact)

- **Built to last**Each item is tested for 6 months before it reaches the shop.
- **Fairly made**We audit our partner factories every year and publish the results.
- **People first**Real humans answer support within a few hours, seven days a week.
